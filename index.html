Case studies

Five companies, from a furniture maker in Cairo to a German agency. Open one to see the problem, what I did, and the proof.

01

Sereno Egypt

Digital Marketing ManagerEgypt · outdoor furniture · 2024–2026
+

The challenge

Sereno sells to homeowners and to hotels, resorts and distributors. Each buys differently, and cheap inquiries were not turning into real orders.

What I did

  • Wrote separate messages and customer journeys for home buyers and hospitality buyers.
  • Moved budget to the sources that converted and paused ad sets that brought cheap, low-quality inquiries.
  • Ran retargeting and audiences built from customer data, and A/B tested messages and designs.
  • Improved the website journey and launched Google Shopping campaigns.
  • Built AskQuo, an AI chat assistant trained on Sereno's own content, as an MVP.

Results

Aseer Time

Regional Social Media Marketing SpecialistSaudi Arabia · Qatar · food & drinks
+

The challenge

One juice and dessert brand, several markets. Each audience needed to hear the brand in its own dialect and around its own occasions.

What I did

  • Wrote captions in Saudi, Qatari and wider Gulf dialects, tied to local life and occasions.
  • Built a content plan of 14 ideas over 6 weeks, with a paid budget of at least $20 a day per account and A/B tests on every piece.
  • Planned the Flamingo range launch in Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
  • Ran Ramadan, Eid and national-day campaigns on Meta and Snapchat, plus influencer collaborations.
  • Designed branch sales contests that reward growth on a single product.

Prolight Media

+

The challenge

Clients in very different sectors, each with its own buying decision, and ad results that only made sense once the tracking behind them was checked.

What I did

  • Wrote client reports with clear next steps, backed by A/B tests, to lower acquisition cost.
